Terms & Conditions

This app is a platform for browsing restaurant menus, placing dine-in or takeaway orders, and reserving a table up to 7 days in advance. It does not offer home delivery. Payment is collected before an order reaches the kitchen or a table reservation is confirmed. Responsibility for food quality, hygiene, preparation, and pricing rests solely with the restaurant preparing and serving the food. Once an order is placed or a reservation is confirmed it cannot be cancelled or refunded through this app — please contact the restaurant directly for any changes. Menu items, prices, and availability are subject to change without prior notice, and an item may be marked out of stock by the restaurant at any time. Please retain your order token and show it at the counter while collecting or being served your order, or quote your name for a table reservation.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what you provide to place your order — your name and phone number — which is used to confirm and prepare your order and for the restaurant's own record-keeping. No data collected through this app is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. This app does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising.
